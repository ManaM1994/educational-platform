# Design System - Based on oteacher.org

## Color Palette
- Primary: Purple/Violet (#7C3AED, #8B5CF6)
- Secondary: Pink/Magenta (#EC4899, #F472B6)
- Accent: Orange (#F97316)
- Background: White (#FFFFFF)
- Gray shades: #F9FAFB, #F3F4F6, #E5E7EB, #6B7280, #374151

## Typography
- Font: Modern sans-serif (similar to Inter/Poppins)
- Headings: Bold, large, clear hierarchy
- Body: Regular weight, good line-height

## Key Design Elements
1. **Gradient backgrounds** - Purple to pink gradients
2. **Rounded corners** - Large border radius (16px-24px)
3. **Soft shadows** - Subtle elevation
4. **Colorful cards** - Vibrant, engaging
5. **Icons** - Prominent, colorful
6. **Spacing** - Generous padding and margins
7. **Buttons** - Large, rounded, gradient or solid colors

## Components Style
- Cards: White background, rounded-2xl, shadow-sm
- Buttons: Rounded-xl, gradient or solid purple/pink
- Inputs: Rounded-lg, border focus with purple
- Navbar: Clean, white, sticky
- Hero: Large gradient background with CTA
